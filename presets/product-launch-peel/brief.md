# Preset · product-launch-peel — reconstruction brief

**Trick:** The trick of this ref is 2–6-frame colour-block bursts and blurred product spins cut on 8ths, alternating with long glossy product holds, while one sentence ("Stick it on everything.") builds word by word over a surface that changes every beat — so a small gadget feels both playful and premium. Family F7 (product) with F6 type.

Reference: `refs/r1_truejigga_claude_vs_katana.mp4`, **top half** (`--region top`), Higgsfield Katana "Peel — the pocket sticker
printer" product launch. Analysis: `refs/analysis/r1_katana/`. 1920×1080 · 30 fps · 20.0 s · ≈122 BPM · 21 shots, a 10-cut burst
between 1.6 and 3.4 s (5–6 frames per cut, a new flat colour each), long product holds after.

<inputs>
Product name (short, 4–5 letters reads best), 3–6 product views (cut-out PNGs or CG/AI stills with soft shadows: hero 3/4,
front, detail, in-use), 4–8 flat cartoon stickers/icons (or small cut-outs), a phone UI capture of the app, 3 surfaces where the
product/result lives (bottle, headphones, laptop…), tagline "The pocket X." Optional: a finger/hand tapping the phone.
</inputs>

<direction>
Playful hardware launch. Warm off-white #F1EFEA for product holds; the burst and the "Stick it" section use saturated flat
backgrounds that change per cut: lime #D6FF3D, cobalt #2D4BFF, hot pink #FF5FB0, orange-red #FF4A1C, black #0E0E10, yellow
#FFD23F. Display type: heavy rounded grotesk sentence case, bottom-left or left placed, with inline black pills holding a tiny
image/icon ("Pick a [◉ photo]"), emphasis in italic serif ("Seconds *later,*" "Stick it on *everything.*"). Logo builds letter by
letter (P → PE → PEE → PEEL) with a peeling corner. Product shots are clean, soft-shadowed, slightly CG. Motion: spins with heavy
directional blur, objects carried across cuts (stickers popping out of the phone), radial smear through spinning stickers.
</direction>

<structure>
0:00.0–0:01.6 / PICK — phone with the app library centre; "Pick a [photo]" → "Pick a [doodle]" → "Pick a [meme]": the pill image and
  the background colour switch on each 8th note (pink sunset, flower doodle, cat meme), finger taps. Kit: phone, words (inline pill),
  BG colour list, cursor (hand).
0:01.6–0:03.4 / SWIPE. — "Swipe." on cobalt; colour-block burst: 2–3-frame cuts of the printer on black, pink, white with a sticker
  card flying out of the phone with tilt. Kit: trans.colorBurst, cutout spin, headline.
0:03.4–0:07.0 / THE PRODUCT — slow 3D-feel orbit around the printer on warm white, button and LED details, "Seconds *later,*"
  (serif, top-left) then a sticker sheet slides out of the slot: "it's [real.]" with the last word in a red box.
  Kit: photo/cutout with Ken Burns + parallax, words (em box).
0:07.0–0:08.8 / PEEL. PRINT. — "Peel." huge on lime with a sticker peeling (corner lift), then "Print." small with the printer on
  white. White flash at 7.0. Kit: headline, cutout, trans.flash.
0:08.8–0:13.4 / STICK IT ON EVERYTHING. — "Stick" → "Stick it" → "Stick it on" → "Stick it on *everything.*" builds bottom-left
  while the object behind changes per beat: laptop on cobalt, bottle on orange (spinning with blur), headphones on black (spin),
  sticker montage on lime; ends with a radial smear through spinning stickers. Kit: words (layout final, bottom-left), spin, BG list,
  trans.smear.
0:13.4–0:15.3 / BURST — sticker pile explodes and re-forms, round "PEEL" badge sticker stamps in. Kit: fan (explode), cutout stamp.
0:15.3–0:18.2 / RIBBON — a long sticker ribbon coils around the printer (or the product wraps in its own output). Kit: spin/photo.
0:18.2–0:20.0 / LOGO — PEEL types on letter by letter in heavy black above the small printer, tagline "The pocket sticker
  printer." fades up small. Kit: typeOn, words.
</structure>

<build>
120–122 BPM grid; the burst uses 1/8-note cuts (0.25 s) then 1/16 (0.125 s). Colour switches are hard cuts on the beat. Each
burst frame carries ONE object. Motion blur on finals. SFX: pop per pill swap, whip per burst cut, hit on "Peel.", whoosh on spins,
riser into the logo, sting on the lockup.
</build>

<gotchas>
Product must look physical (soft contact shadows, no floating flat cards). Burst frames 4–6 frames each, never shorter than 3.
9:16: type bottom-left lands above the 420 px bottom safe zone; product centred upper-middle.
</gotchas>
