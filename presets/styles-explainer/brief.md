**Trick:** The trick of this ref is one fixed chapter template (/style title · 01/8 counter · BEFORE → AFTER) repeated six times at ≈1.9 s, so each look reads instantly and the variety itself sells the tool. Family F6 with F2 portraits.

Reference: `refs/r3_zainoldinn_katana_launch.mp4` (full frame), Higgsfield × Claude Motion "Motion design skills" explainer.
Analysis: `refs/analysis/r3/`. 1440×1080 · 24 fps · 24.0 s · ≈150 BPM feel (onsets every 0.4 s; the detector reports 97.8 —
use 150 for cutting) · six style chapters of ≈1.9 s, each BEFORE → AFTER.
